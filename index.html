What is Nexora? ＋

Nexora is a concept project-management workspace designed to bring tasks, projects, team coordination, and progress visibility into one place.

Can Nexora work for a small team? ＋

Yes. The concept is designed to scale from a small group to teams with multiple projects and workflows.

Do I need to change how my team works? ＋

The goal is to adapt the workspace to your process, with clear projects, flexible tasks, and simple collaboration.
